Union Ambassador Nilan Bannerjee has been on a diplomatic mission to Cressidium, a planet that was settled by Union’s anthrochauvinistic Second Committee during the Second Expansion Period. 

This is Union's third visit to the planet since the Third Committee was founded and communication with the planet re-established. Ties with union had been deliberately severed  by SecComm Loyalists who fled to the planet to avoid prosecution following the civil war. Scuttling the local omninet infrastructure, including the nearby blinkgate station, Loyalists established the cradle-like world as a haven for them and their political allies. The loss of Cressidium from official record during the chaos meant that the planet was left effectively isolated for the better part of a millennium. During this time, native populations were pushed to the fringes as authoritarian governments rose and fell. Now, after being rediscovered by a union survey team, the planet is negotiating a return into the fold. 

Ambassador Bannerjee has been hard at work with negotiations, but tensions between major factions on Cressidium has caused negotiations to stall. Two of these factions, the Leandric States Alliance (LSA) and the Vestan Sovereignty, have been locked in a cold war for generations. The LSA, first to make contact with the colonial
survey team, has been very receptive to the possibility of becoming a Union member state once more, while the Sovereignty is much more distrustful. The LSA port city of Nov Elysia has served as host to the ongoing diplomatic summits and Ambassador Bannerjee has spent countless hours trying to find a compromise acceptable to all parties. 

You have been assigned to the UNS-CV Rio Grande, an Ebro-class light carrier under command of Captain Brigid Farris that has served as escort to the small fleet of Union delegates. Your assignment is to assist the Rio Grande in ensuring the security of the diplomatic efforts on the planet. Ambassador Bannerjee has recruited the help of Captain Farris in hopes that a member of the Union Navy could provide a fresh perspective during negotiations, and the UAD has seen it fit to bolster naval forces aboard the Rio Grande. Stay fresh and stay alert, Lancer.
